FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a credit card for the free trial?
+
No! Start your 14-day trial with just an email address. No credit card required. You only enter payment details when you're ready to upgrade to a paid plan.
Can I import my existing data?
+
Yes! Upload products and sales via CSV or Excel files. We support Tally exports, Kaggle retail datasets, and custom formats. Our smart column mapper handles mismatched headers automatically.
Do you support multiple stores?
+
Absolutely! Manage 1 to 50 stores from one dashboard. Filter data by store, compare performance across locations, and assign managers to specific stores with role-based access.
Is my data secure?
+
100%. We use bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption, daily automated backups, and host on Supabase (SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure). Your data is never shared with third parties.
Do you have a mobile app?
+
Android app is launching in Q3 2026 with full dashboard, barcode scanning, and push notifications. iOS app follows in Q4 2026. Currently, the web app is fully responsive and works great on mobile browsers.
Can I cancel anytime?
+
Yes! No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel with one click from your account settings. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and can export all your data anytime.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and wallets via Razorpay. International payments via Stripe for Southeast Asia and Middle East customers. Annual plans can be paid via bank transfer.
What if I need help getting started?
+
We offer email support (all plans), priority chat support (Professional+), and dedicated onboarding calls (Enterprise). Plus a comprehensive help centre with video tutorials, guides, and sample datasets.
